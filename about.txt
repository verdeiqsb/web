VerdeIQ Sdn Bhd: Engineering Tomorrow’s Ecosystems Today

1. Executive Summary
VerdeIQ Sdn Bhd is a Malaysian-based innovation company specializing in intelligent systems for agriculture, irrigation, flood mitigation, environmental monitoring, and facility management. By integrating IoT, AI, and cloud-native platforms, VerdeIQ transforms traditional infrastructure into smart, adaptive ecosystems. Its mission is to not only monitor but optimize environments—urban, rural, and industrial—through data-driven automation and predictive intelligence.

3. Core Innovation Domains
🌾 Agriculture & Irrigation Systems
- Smart irrigation using soil moisture sensors, evapotranspiration models, and weather forecasting
- AI-driven crop yield prediction and pest detection
- Fertigation control with nutrient dosing automation
- Remote pump and valve control via MQTT and LoRaWAN
- Integration with farm management platforms and satellite imagery
🌊 Flood Mitigation & Water Management
- Real-time flood prediction using rainfall telemetry, river levels, and terrain modeling
- IoT-based drainage control with automated sluice gates and pump stations
- Urban flood modeling using AI and GIS
- Community alert systems via SMS, sirens, and mobile apps
- Integration with municipal SCADA and emergency response systems
🌱 Environmental Monitoring
- Air quality sensors (PM2.5, CO2, VOCs, NOx) with real-time dashboards
- Water quality sensors (pH, turbidity, conductivity, heavy metals)
- Noise pollution mapping and alerting
- ESG compliance reporting and carbon footprint tracking
- Forest fire detection using thermal imaging and satellite feeds
🏢 Building & Facility Management
- Smart HVAC systems with occupancy-based control and predictive maintenance
- Cleanroom automation: pressure differential, humidity, particle count
- Energy optimization using AI load forecasting and peak shaving
- Leak detection, access control, and fire safety integration
- Unified dashboards for multi-site facility control
⚡ Electrical & Infrastructure Systems
- Load balancing and demand response systems
- Predictive maintenance for transformers, switchgear, and UPS
- Solar and battery integration with grid-tied and off-grid modes
- Power quality monitoring (harmonics, voltage dips, flicker)
- Facility-wide energy dashboards with anomaly detection
