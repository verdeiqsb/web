Executive Summary

VerdeIQ Sdn Bhd is a Malaysian-based innovation company specializing in intelligent systems for agriculture, irrigation, flood mitigation, environmental monitoring, and facility management. By integrating IoT, AI, and cloud-native platforms, VerdeIQ transforms traditional infrastructure into smart, adaptive ecosystems. Its mission is to not only monitor but optimize environments—urban, rural, and industrial—through data-driven automation and predictive intelligence.
