Philosophy
VerdeIQ is built on the belief that engineering, automation, and artificial intelligence can reshape industries while protecting the planet. We aspire to deliver forward-thinking IoT solutions that balance innovation with sustainability, ensuring long-term value for communities and businesses alike.

Legacy & Scope

Since its inception, VerdeIQ has contributed to advanced facility systems including FMCS cleanroom solutions, BMS/SCADA integration, and FFU/APC monitoring platforms. Our business scope spans consultation and engineering services — from design and development to installation, inspection, maintenance, and testing. We also engage in global import/export and trading of specialized equipment, appliances, components, and intelligent control devices, ensuring clients benefit from cutting-edge technologies and reliable support.

Research & Upcoming Solutions

Research Focus: Integrating machine learning and AI into industrial systems, fostering urban farming partnerships, and developing automated flood management and environmental control frameworks.

Upcoming Solutions: Predictive tooling for production plants, smart urban farm platforms, and integrated flood management with environmental monitoring and irrigation systems — all designed to enhance resilience and sustainability.

Executive Summary

VerdeIQ Sdn Bhd is a Malaysian innovation company specializing in digitalization, industrial automation, and intelligent building systems. Our solutions span environmental and facility management, agriculture, irrigation, and flood mitigation. By harnessing cloud-native platforms, IoT, and AI, VerdeIQ transforms traditional infrastructure into adaptive, data-driven ecosystems. Our mission is to not only monitor but actively optimize urban, rural, and industrial environments — creating smarter, more sustainable futures through predictive intelligence and automation.
